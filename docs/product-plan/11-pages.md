# 11 — Pages

One entry per page: what it shows to a **free**, **trial**, **paid** and
**ended** student (ended = the trial or a package has ended and nothing
else is live), and which decisions land on it. A page's entry is
completed when its redesign starts, not before. What is here now is
what is already ruled for that page, plus **Check** — small faults on
today's page to look at then, most of which the redesign will remove.

The rules themselves live in the feature files; this file only says
where they show.

## Student

One menu for every status; a locked item carries a lock and says what
opens it (00, The menu).

### Dashboard
- **Where sign-in lands, for every status; its top card is this
  student's next step** (Sam, 2026-10-04: A) — free: today's challenge
  and the rank line; trial: days left, carry on, today's challenge;
  paid: carry on, today's challenge; ended: the recap card.
- Free: "Free: practice on the free set" (03 F2).
- Trial: a bar — "Trial, limited access — N days left" — from day one,
  amber in the last three days, with Choose a package (03).
- Ended: **a recap card** — the trial in numbers (questions answered, %
  correct, quizzes done), up to three topics to work on, the percentile
  on the trial paper (05), Choose a package; it stays while nothing is
  live, no storage. When a package ends, the same card with "Your
  access ended on …".
- Paid with only a future access row: "RN Full access starts 13 Oct
  2026" in place of an offer (02).
- The announcement strip (08); the last five sittings; accuracy and the
  trend (05 Q12); the streak and questions mastered — where, open (05).

### My courses (sidebar) and the course page
- Ended: the courses stay, marked locked; the course page shows what it
  holds and what the student did in it, locked (03).
- The course's announcements; "Message us" opening a draft about the
  course (08); days left from the stored end (02).

### Practice papers
- The name and "Practice Paper N", the course in full above (04 Q21);
  one section per allowed mode; UPCOMING / CLOSED by the server's clock;
  a paper holding a draft question refuses to start, with the message on
  the card.
- Trial: the ticked paper first, "Included in your trial"; the others
  locked — "Opens with a package", Choose a package (03).
- Ended: every paper listed, the trial paper with its score, the rest
  locked; at the top "Your trial ended on 18 October. Your scores are
  still here."

### Mock exams
- Free, trial, ended, and paid without Premium Prep: listed, locked,
  with the way to Premium Prep — the words at the redesign (02.10;
  Start refused today with "Mock exams aren't included in your
  package.").
- Paid with Premium Prep: open (02.10).

### Quiz builder
- Free: pick a course, build from its free questions (03 F1).
- Trial: three quizzes in all. Ended: "You've used your 3 trial
  quizzes", locked.
- Four mode cards under Study and Exam; the Topics step can open with a
  course and topic already set (from the report).
- **Check**: the status line sticks after a failure; picking another
  course leaves the previous course's topics and counts; an "All
  topics" quiz is named after the course's first three topics.

### Daily challenge
- The first visit brings the username card: an animal and a word
  already filled, one tap to accept, the animal grid, up to 3 spins of
  the word (05).

### The sitting — start screen, sitting, review
- The start screen alone; Start or Resume; a resumed exam's minutes
  left; per-mode "Don't show this again" (04).
- Hide / Show timer; Sequential's Next stays off until answered, no
  Previous; the review moves freely. Submitting with flags: "You still
  have N flagged questions" — Go back and review / Submit anyway; with
  blanks: "You have N unanswered questions" — Go back / Submit anyway.
  No leave-page warning after Save & Resume Later or Submit & Exit.
- Report this question (06). The admin's preview says Preview (04 Q19).
- Ended: an unfinished sitting finishes; the review is locked; Retake
  kept with a lock — "Choose a package to retake" (03).
- **Check**: a resumed exam's brief still opens with "The clock starts
  when you begin…" before "The clock is still running".

### Results pop-up and the report
- As in 04. On the report the sidebar marks Learning History.
- Ended: the summary open; the question list locked — "Choose a package
  to review your answers". The percentile on the trial paper's report
  (05).

### Learning history
- Every sitting under its course's name; a Report link on finished
  ones; mode chips from the mode table; the true totals and topic

- **Check**: for a student with no live course the names show as codes

  only the loaded pages; no Mock option in Source, and a mock shows the
  raw word "mock".
