1. The decisions (Sam, 2026-10-05)

1.1 The free account stays the floor; its public name is "Free forever"

  • Confirms 4 October's two ways in, one floor: registering starts the 14-day trial, buying a package makes the account, and every student lands on the free account when a trial or package ends.
  • Readers see it as Free forever. "Free account" stays the plan's working word.
  • No Free card on the home page, and no button anywhere says "free account". The only way-in wording is Start your 14-day trial.
  • Why: Claude's plans page (Free, Pro, Max) was the prompt. Claude's Free is a tier that never ends, which matches our floor, not our trial; the trial is a 14-day head start. Calling the trial "Free" would make "Free" shrink on day 15. Claude can keep Free forever because every chat is new; a question bank is used up once seen, so the forever part stays the free set, not the real bank.

1.2 The home page plans row is design C

  • Three cards: Trial, Standard Prep, Premium Prep, under the heading "Start your 14-day trial, then free practice, forever".
  • The trial card carries the full Free forever box (four lines), then "Plus, for 14 days", the only filled button ("Start your 14-day trial") and "After 14 days, Free forever stays."
  • Standard Prep and Premium Prep each carry a one-line strip: "Free forever included, and kept when the package ends", then what is paid for, and an outlined button.
  • Under the row, the single-course line (1.4).
  • Why C over A and B: A's Free card has no button, so on a phone the first card is a dead end. B prints the free list three times, makes the phone about 2,600px long against C's 2,100, and puts free things ahead of what a package is paid for. C keeps the trial first, says the free list once, and lets the paid cards lead with what they sell. If a card is ever shown alone (the Premium Prep page, a screenshot), use B's full box there.

1.3 Full Access is renamed Standard Prep

  • Standard Prep: all your programme's courses for 365 days. Premium Prep: the same, plus mock exams and the premium channel, for 240 days.
  • Why: "Full" promised everything, but Premium Prep has more. Standard and Premium read as a pair, and the shared "Prep" says they are the same kind of thing.
  • Considered and passed over: Full Year (true, but Sam was unsure); Regular Prep (in Ghanaian education "regular" means full-time students, against sandwich and distance); Standard / Premium Access ("Access" only says a door opens, every package is access, it carries the old Full Access confusion, and Premium Prep already exists with its page and channel); anything with Pass (reads as a promise to pass the NMC exam).
  • Standard fits either price plan Sam has in mind: both the same price (Standard's card then shows "for 365 days" so the choice is more time or mocks and the channel) or Premium higher (Standard is simply the cheaper way in).
  • Package names are labels edited in the admin; package ids do not change.

1.4 Single courses come as Standard Prep or Premium Prep

  • A single course can be bought as Standard Prep or Premium Prep, with the same days as the related prep: 365 and 240.
  • Names: "<Course> Standard Prep" and "<Course> Premium Prep", e.g. "Midwifery Standard Prep". Today they are "<Course> Standalone Access".
  • A Premium single course opens that course's mock exams, and its buyer joins the programme's premium channel.
  • No code change: ticking premium on a package already turns its mocks on (02.10) and lists it on the Premium Prep page. The channel's bot (17, not built) must count a Premium single course as live Premium Prep.
  • The card line: "Only need one course? Take it on its own, as Standard Prep or Premium Prep, from GHS [PRICE]." with a link, See single courses.

1.5 Smaller calls

  • Quiz Builder keeps its name. Customised Practice Builder, Practice Builder and Custom Practice were weighed and left.
  • Free Full Access stays as it is: the admin's grant, not sold. (A student holding one does see its name on the dashboard's "Then … until …" line; renaming it later is one field in the admin.)
  • The cards say "All your programme's courses" and never single out the General Paper.

Trial: 14 days from registering

  • All the programme's courses.
  • One practice paper per course: the one ticked "Open in trial". A course with no ticked paper opens none.
  • 3 Quiz Builder quizzes in all, from the full bank, up to 50 questions each; retakes do not count.
  • All four modes, Study and Exam. Full review and retakes while the trial lasts.
  • No mock exams, no offline packs.
  • The phone number checked by SMS before it starts; one trial per number. to build F4b

Standard Prep (today "Full Access")

  • All the programme's courses, 365 days.
  • Every practice paper. Unlimited Quiz Builder, up to 50 questions each. All four modes.
  • Offline packs: 5 per course, up to 100 questions each.
  • Full review and retakes.
  • No mock exams, no premium channel.

Premium Prep

  • Everything in Standard Prep, the same courses, for 240 days.
  • Mock exams built (the gate, 02.10; the mocks' own design, Q3, is parked).
  • The premium channel: a private Telegram channel per programme; Sam posts, members read; joined through the bot, removed when Premium Prep ends. to build 17

Single courses

  • One course, as Standard Prep (365 days) or Premium Prep (240 days), with what that tier gives, for that course (1.4).

4. Found while checking

The free set is empty on dev. 0 of about 5,600 published questions are marked free (is_free_sample), in every course. Free forever and the daily challenge have nothing to draw from until questions are marked. This is authoring work before launch.

  • Dev's trial papers: 5 practice papers on dev, one each in GP, RM_MID, RM_PED_OBS_HRN, RN_MED and RN_SURG; only the General Paper's is ticked "Open in trial". Content on prod may differ.
  • Dev's prices (parked, not final): Full Access GHS 150 for 365 days; Premium Prep GHS 99 for 240 days; single courses GHS 59 for 365 days. On these, Premium is cheaper than Standard while giving more; Sam plans either equal prices or Premium higher.
  • Every programme's package holds its two courses plus the General Paper (RN: GP, RN_MED, RN_SURG), the trial the same.
  • Config: builder up to 50 questions (40 by default); a pack up to 100; 5 packs per course.
  • Hand-typed "Full Access" in public copy: app/premium-prep/page.tsx (the lede, the "Not sitting the exam this year?" note, the metadata), app/subscribe/page.tsx (line 211), app/page.tsx (line 190). These go with 02.11 / 02.12 and the redesign.
