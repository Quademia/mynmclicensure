1. The question and the short answer

Sam asked: what is the loading thing we chose called? Should we have built our own instead (the NMC Ghana logo, our logo and the app's name), or can we add our own on top of it? Some apps at Sam's work show their name while a page loads.

  • Its name: a skeleton screen (also "skeleton loader"): grey shapes in the page's layout, pulsing gently, until the real page arrives.
  • Should we have built our own instead? No, not for moving between pages. The skeleton costs almost no data (no pictures), keeps the top bar and the menu still, and the pages arrive in about half a second once it shows; a logo animation would only flash.
  • What the work apps show is a splash screen: shown once when the app opens, not on every page. It sits beside the skeleton, not in place of it.
  • Can we add our own on top? Yes. Five ways were offered:
OptionWhat it isOutcome
1. A welcome screen when the app opensThe Q and the name, onceaccepted — design 1
2. Branded long waitsA Q-and-message screen for the waits of secondsaccepted — design 2
3. A small Q in the skeletonFor the two pages with no top barnot taken
4. A branded progress barA thin teal line along the topnot taken
5. A full-screen logo on every pageIn place of the skeletonadvised against

About the logos

  • The NMC Ghana logo: advised against, unless NMC gives written permission. It is the regulator's logo; in a prep app it can look like NMC endorses us, which is a trademark risk. Using the words "NMC Ghana" in a description, as the app does, is different.
  • "Our logo and the app's name": the rules say the brand is Quademia and MyNMCLicensure has no logo of its own (AGENTS.md, UI rule 5). The Quademia Q with "MyNMCLicensure" beside it is within that rule; a separate MyNMCLicensure logo would change it.

3. The two designs

Design 1 — the welcome screen

  • When: once, when the app opens (the installed app's icon opens /router) and right after signing in. Never again in that visit.
  • What: the Q; QUADEMIA in small capitals; MyNMCLicensure; "NMC Ghana licensure exam prep"; a thin moving line at the bottom.
  • The phone's own screen comes first. Android draws a screen of its own from the app's settings (app/manifest.ts) before the app starts; an iPhone shows plain white. The Q in design 1 sits where Android puts it, so the two read as one screen.
  • Drawn in two colours: 1B white and 1C navy.

Design 2 — the "please wait" screen

  • When: only for waits of several seconds that hold the whole screen. Never for an ordinary move between pages.
  • What: the Q inside a spinning teal ring, one line saying what is happening, the name at the bottom. The look is the same everywhere; only the message changes:
WhereMessage
Starting a quiz"Loading your quiz…"today's words
Save & Exit in a quiz"Saving your progress…"today's words
Submit & Exit in a quiz"Quiz submitted."today's words
Coming back from Paystack"Confirming your payment…" and "This can take a few seconds."new — proposed (no screen there today)
Downloading a pack's PDF"Making your PDF…"new — proposed (today only the button says "Making…")
Any wait past about 8 seconds"Still loading — your connection is slow. Please keep this page open."new — proposed

  • Design 1 accepted; 1C, navy, chosen. Going navy means the phone's own opening screen turns navy too, so the two match (the manifest's background_color; the bar colour, theme_color, stays white to match the top bar — Claude's recommendation, not yet put to Sam).

  • The skeleton stays for every move between pages. Neither design replaces it.

5. The "please wait" screen as a shared piece

So that it is not overused, one rule for which kind of waiting goes where:

What is happeningWhat the student sees
Moving to another pageThe skeleton — automatic
A quick action, a second or twoThe button's words change ("Start" → "Starting…") — the app does this already
A wait of several seconds that holds the screenThe "please wait" screen with its message
Any wait past about 8 secondsThe slow-connection note beneath it, if Sam says yes

When it is built, it goes in three places, so it is used every time without anyone asking:

  1. The code: one shared component every wait uses.
  2. The design system: a line under Shared pieces in 09-design-system.md, and a card on the MyNMCLicensure design system on Claude.
  3. The rules: a line in AGENTS.md's UI conventions — "a wait of seconds uses the please-wait screen".

8. Found while checking

  • Coming back from Paystack shows nothing. app/payment-confirmation/ has no loading.tsx, so while the payment is checked the student most likely sees Paystack's page sit there. Read in the code, not walked. Who it reaches: every paying student once this app is live; today only dev, since the live product is still the old site.
  • A bookmark skips the welcome screen. Opening the installed app always passes /router; a bookmark straight to a page (say, Courses) goes to that page.
  • The full Q is 91 KB (public/images/quademia-mark.png). The screens should use the 18 KB copy (quademia-mark-192.png) so they show on a weak signal.
  • iPhone's own opening screen is white. With a navy welcome screen, an iPhone would flash white first unless start-up images are added for it.
  • The hand-over to the dashboard must be checked when building: the welcome screen should give way to the dashboard with no skeleton flashing in between (see AGENTS.md, Under a loading.tsx, redirect() and notFound() answer 200).
