Features

Everything you need to assess and improve

MyTeacher gives educators the tools to run rigorous assessments without the administrative overhead.

Classes & enrolment

Create classes, share a join code with students, and manage enrolment with an approval flow. Full control over who's in your class.

Question bank

Author MCQ, True/False, and SATA questions. Import in bulk via CSV or use AI-assisted creation to build your bank faster.

Quiz engine

Set time limits, assign quizzes to specific classes, use access codes, and shuffle questions or answers to reduce copying.

Results & analytics

View marksheets, run item analysis to identify weak questions, and control when students can see their results — per class.

Academic structure

Organise content by programme, cohort, and course. Mirrors the way health training institutions are actually structured.

Student experience

Students join by code, take quizzes on any device, and review their answers once results are released — with no friction.
